The source file is in Source/, unchanged. Livery: Black, as the source has it.
The other models in these folders are made from it by Tools/Blender/outpiston.py. Changes: split into body, truck and wheelset;
turned to run along Y and moved so the rail is at zero; one material per texture; its own couplers taken off; each texture's roughness set to suit worn paint and grimy running gear;
the headlights, ditch lights, horn and bell cut out as parts of their own. Its couplers
are the shared one in Models/Couplers, from the SD40 (see the CREDITS.txt there).

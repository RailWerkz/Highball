The original files are in Source (Locomotive_FBX.fbx and its textures in Source/CSX-max), unchanged.
The other models in these folders are made from it by Tools/Blender/ac4400cw.py. Changes: everything but the running gear
baked into one body, the trucks and wheelsets kept apart; turned to run along Y and moved so the rail is at zero; the
running gear squeezed across to standard gauge (the source's is about 1.73 m) and centred under the body; coplanar faces
merged and dense parts reduced (4.7 million triangles to about 1 million); every material made non-metallic but the wheel
treads and lamp reflectors, the bump maps turned into normal maps, the opacity maps laid into the colour's alpha, the glass
kept as a mesh of its own (Glass), see-through at the source's opacity, the maps cut to 2048 and written as JPEG; empty nodes for the
headlights, ditch lights, horn, exhaust and bell (the source has no bell). Its couplers are the shared one in Models/Couplers, from the SD40 (see the CREDITS.txt there).

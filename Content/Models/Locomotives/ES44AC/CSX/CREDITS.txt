The original files are in Source/Mesh and Source/Texture, unchanged.
Source/gevo_es44ac.glb and .blend are made from Source/Mesh/Gevo.blend by Tools/Blender/gevo_textures.py: the geometry
unchanged, its three materials (Gevo, Gevo CAB, Control) given the Diffuse, Normal, Spec and Ao textures, which the original
pointed at UV checker images that weren't included.
The other models in these folders are made from that .blend by Tools/Blender/gevo.py. Changes: split into body, truck and
wheelset; turned to run along Y and moved so the rail is at zero; the occlusion laid into the colour, the maps cut to 2048
and written as JPEG, one roughness per material; the headlights, ditch lights and horn cut out as parts of their own, and
an empty node for the bell (the source has none). Its couplers are the shared one in Models/Couplers, from the SD40 (see the
CREDITS.txt there).

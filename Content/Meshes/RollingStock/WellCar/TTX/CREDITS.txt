The original files are in Source/Green-obj (Green.obj, Green.mtl, Car.jpg, Green.jpg, Yang.jpg, Reflection.hdr), unchanged,
unpacked from Meshes/RollingStock/wellcar_doublestack/Green-obj.rar. The other models in these folders are made from them by
Tools/Blender/wellcar_doublestack.py. Changes: scaled (about 0.072 m a unit) so the well's inside is the 53 ft 1-3/4 in
lettered on the car; everything but the running gear and the containers baked into one body, the trucks, wheelsets and
the two containers kept apart; moved so the rail is at zero; the running gear squeezed across to standard gauge and centred
under the body; its couplers taken off; the ground plane dropped; the faces of the objects built inside out turned outward
and the normals worked out afresh; the lettering on the side girders and the containers' sides and doors, mapped a quarter
turned in the source, mapped flat; the containers stretched a little to their ISO sizes (40 ft by 8 ft, 8 ft 6 in and
9 ft 6 in high); coplanar faces merged and dense parts reduced (1,280,000 triangles to about 172,000 with both boxes);
every material made non-metallic but the wheel treads, the car's texture cut to 4096 and the containers' to 2048, written as
JPEG. Its couplers are the shared one in Meshes/Couplers, from the SD40 (see the CREDITS.txt there).
